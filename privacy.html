Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 November 2026

This explains what GARO AI collects, why, who helps us process it, and your choices. We do not sell your data and we do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

1. What we collect

  • Account: your email, name, date of birth (to enforce the 16+ rule and for optional birthday greetings), and phone number. Your phone number is private: other users never see it.
  • Profile: display name, username, profile picture, short bio and preferences. Other people see the name, username, picture and bio only if you share a group with them.
  • Content: your chats, group messages, private messages, files, pictures and generated pictures, and messages to Website Administration.
  • Usage and safety records: counts that enforce daily limits, sign-in events, reports, and the actions of administrators. We do not collect IP addresses in the app.

2. Who processes it

  • Supabase: sign-in, database and file storage.
  • Oracle Cloud: GARO AI's private server that runs the text AI models, the search service and the picture gateway.
  • Cloudflare Workers AI: used only for the two picture features. When you use Generate Image, your description is sent to FLUX.1 Schnell. When you press send with a picture attached, or use Understand Image, that picture (reduced in size) and your question are sent to Moondream 3.1. These requests always go through GARO AI's server. Cloudflare's own terms and privacy policy apply to what it processes. Pictures you share with other people are never sent to Cloudflare.

3. How we use it

To run Garo: sign you in, save your chats, deliver messages, show pictures, enforce limits and the rules, keep the service safe, give support, and send you notices you ask for. We do not use your content to show ads.

4. Who can see your content

  • Your chats with Garo: only you, and administrators where needed for security, abuse handling, support or legal reasons.
  • Group messages and files: the members of that group.
  • Private messages: only the two people in them. Other members and group admins cannot read them.
  • View once pictures: the receiver can open them one time. They are locked after opening and erased from storage about ten minutes later, or after 14 days if never opened.

5. How long we keep it

Until you delete it or your account. Deleting your account erases your account, chats and group messages. Files can be removed by their owner or an admin. Daily limit counters and security records are kept only as long as needed to run and protect the service.

6. Cookies and local storage

  • Essential: keeps you signed in and remembers your cookie choice. Always on.
  • Preferences (only if you allow them): theme, last model, recent emojis, welcome screens and reminders. We also keep a message in your browser's storage for a short time if your connection drops before it is sent, so it can be sent again.
  • Advertising and tracking: not used. You can change your choice any time from Cookie settings.

7. Your choices

You can edit your profile, change or remove your picture, delete messages you sent, block people, turn off notifications, and delete your account from your profile. To ask for a copy of your data or for help, contact us below.

8. Children

Garo is for people aged 16 and over. We block sign-ups under 16. If you think a younger child has an account, tell us and we will remove it.

9. Security

Access to data is limited by per-user rules in the database, keys for the picture models are kept on the server and never in your browser, and pictures use private storage. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password.

10. Changes

When we change this policy the date above changes and we may tell you in the app.
