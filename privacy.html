Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

This explains what GARO AI collects, why, who helps us process it, and your choices. We do not sell your data and we do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

1. What we collect

  • Account: your email, name, date of birth (to enforce the 16+ rule and for optional birthday greetings), and phone number. Your phone number is private: other users never see it.
  • Profile: display name, username, profile picture, short bio and preferences. Other people see the name, username, picture and bio only if you share a group with them.
  • Content: your chats, group messages, private messages, files, pictures and generated pictures, and messages to Administrator.
  • Answer feedback: if you press thumbs up or thumbs down on an AI answer, that choice is saved with your chat (for AI answers in groups, in your browser only). If you press thumbs down, you may also choose to send a short report. It is sent to the Administrator only when you press Send, and it contains your question and that answer. If you press Skip, nothing is sent.
  • Usage and safety records: counts that enforce daily limits, sign-in events, reports, and the actions of administrators. For the two picture features we also record which feature was used, by which account and when, so the shared daily allowance stays fair. We do not collect IP addresses in the app.

2. Who processes it

  • Supabase: sign-in, database and file storage.
  • Football data for Betalytica: public results from football-data.co.uk, team ratings from ClubElo, and optionally crests from football-data.org. Only team names are sent to these services, never your account details. Web research uses our own SearXNG/YaCy services, and the Qwen AI model on our server reviews the evidence.
  • Oracle Cloud: GARO AI's private server that runs the text AI models, the search service and the picture gateway.
  • Web search (our own server): to answer with current facts, Garo sends a short search query taken from your message (up to 200 characters, with email addresses, phone numbers and long key-like codes removed) to search services that run on GARO AI's server. They ask public search engines for results and may open and read public web pages. We do not send your account details, and greetings and very short messages are not searched. Public search engines receive only that short query.
  • Cloudflare Workers AI: used only for the two picture features. When you use Generate Image, your description is sent to FLUX.1 Schnell. When you press send with a picture attached, or use Understand Image, that picture (reduced in size) and your question are sent to Moondream 3.1. These requests always go through GARO AI's server. Cloudflare's own terms and privacy policy apply to what it processes. Pictures you share with other people are never sent to Cloudflare.

3. Voice features: read aloud and dictation

  • Read aloud uses the text-to-speech built into your browser and device. Garo does not record, receive or store any audio. Some browsers use online voices run by the browser's maker; their own privacy policy then applies to the text they read.
  • Dictation (the microphone button) uses your browser's speech recognition. Depending on your browser, your voice may be sent to the browser maker's speech service to be turned into text; Garo itself never receives or stores your audio, only the text you choose to send. The microphone is used only while you have dictation switched on, and your browser asks for permission first.
  • Transcribing an audio file runs on your own device. The first time, your browser downloads a small speech model from a public code library network.
  • Share on an AI answer opens your device's share menu, or copies the text so you can paste it. Garo does not see where you share it.

4. How we use it

To run Garo: sign you in, save your chats, deliver messages, show pictures, enforce limits and the rules, keep the service safe, give support, and send you notices you ask for. We do not use your content to show ads.

5. Who can see your content

  • To run support and keep Garo safe, the Administrator can see account status, sign-in history (times, browser and device), usage counts and file sizes, add private notes to an account, and suspend accounts that break the Terms. The Administrator cannot see your password.
  • While you use Garo, your browser tells us you are online (about once a minute) so the Administrator can see how many people are active. If you add your birthday, Garo sends you a greeting on that day; the Administrator sees only counts and the greeting sent, not your birthday.
  • AI conversations: the Administrator can open the AI chats you save in Garo (not Sneak Peek chats, which are not stored) to keep Garo safe, prevent abuse and give support. Every conversation opened or searched is recorded in an audit log with the time. Attached document text is not shown in that view. Do not put secrets in chats.
  • Betalytica (football analysis, 18+): the teams you analyse, the results, and your saved analyses and favourite teams are stored in your account. The Administrator can see these and the technical record of how each result was made.
  • Your email, display name, username and phone number (from sign-up or your profile) are kept in a user directory that only the Administrator can view, to run support and the Administrator chat.
  • Group invites can be joined by scanning a group QR code with your camera. The camera image is processed on your device and is not uploaded.
  • Search inside Garo: it only looks through your own chats, the groups you are in, your private messages and your Administrator chat. You can never find other people's content, and view once pictures are never searched.
  • The Administrator's name and picture are chosen by the Administrator and shown to people who chat with the Administrator. The Administrator receives support messages from users in an inbox and cannot send a support message to themselves.
  • Your chats with Garo: only you, and administrators where needed for security, abuse handling, support or legal reasons.
  • Group messages and files: the members of that group.
  • Private messages: only the two people in them. Other members and group admins cannot read them.
  • View once pictures: the receiver can open them one time. They are locked after opening and erased from storage about ten minutes later, or after 14 days if never opened.

6. How long we keep it

Until you delete it or your account. Deleting your account erases your account, chats and group messages. Files can be removed by their owner or an admin. Daily limit counters and security records are kept only as long as needed to run and protect the service.

7. Cookies and local storage

  • Essential: keeps you signed in and remembers your cookie choice. Always on.
  • Preferences (only if you allow them): theme, last model, recent emojis, welcome screens, reminders and your thumbs up or down on AI answers in groups. We also keep a message in your browser's storage for a short time if your connection drops before it is sent, so it can be sent again.
  • Advertising and tracking: not used. You can change your choice any time from Cookie settings.

8. Your choices

You can edit your profile, change or remove your picture, delete messages you sent, block people, turn off notifications, and delete your account from your profile. To ask for a copy of your data or for help, contact us below.

9. Children

Garo is for people aged 16 and over. We block sign-ups under 16. If you think a younger child has an account, tell us and we will remove it.

10. Security

Access to data is limited by per-user rules in the database, keys for the picture models are kept on the server and never in your browser, and pictures use private storage. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password.

11. Changes

When we change this policy the date above changes and we may tell you in the app.
