Questions

Straight answers.

Who is Garo?

Garo is the AI assistant of Divine-Ollama. It runs on a private server that Divine-Ollama operates, so your conversations are not sent to big AI companies.

Who is the owner of Divine-Ollama (Garo AI)?

Divine-Ollama and Garo AI are owned by Divinewill Gopeh Inyang, also known as Divine Gopeh: a young Nigerian web developer who studied at the University of the People. Born on 24 October 2003. Dark in complexion.

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no per-message charges. It runs on a free server, so it is shared and has fair-use limits.

Is the chat unlimited?

There is no message cap, but the server has limited power. When several people chat at once, replies queue, and each account has a per-minute limit to keep things fair.

Can Garo read or create images?

Not yet. For now Garo focuses on text, documents and code. Image features may come later once the server can handle them.

What is Developers mode?

It is a shared group where several people build one project together. Anyone can ask Garo to build or change files, save the result to the project, preview it, and download it. Admins can add people, lock the group and manage roles.

How does Deep Research cite my documents?

You add the documents and, if you like, each one's author, year and title. Garo writes from the documents and the app adds the in-text citations and the reference list in the style you choose. Without documents, Garo does standard research and will not invent references.

Is Garo up to date?

Garo's built-in knowledge stops at a cutoff date. Turn on Web search from the + menu, or press "Verify with the web", and Garo checks current sources through a private search service and shows them as links. It also searches automatically when you tell it an answer is wrong or outdated.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, any time, from your profile. It erases your account, chats and group messages. Accounts that break the Terms of Use can be suspended or deleted by an administrator.

Who can see my chats?

Other users cannot. Chats are stored in your account with per-user access rules. The administrator can technically reach stored data for security and support. See the Privacy Policy.
