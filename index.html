Questions

Straight answers.

Who is Garo?

Garo is the private AI assistant of GARO AI. Your text conversations run on a private server that GARO AI operates, so they are not sent to big AI companies. Only when you use Generate Image or Understand Image, that description or picture is processed by Cloudflare Workers AI through GARO AI's server.

Who is the owner of GARO AI?

GARO AI is owned by Divinewill Gopeh Inyang, also known as Divine Gopeh: a young Nigerian web developer who studied at the University of the People. Born on 24 October 2003. Dark in complexion.

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no per-message charges. It runs on a free server, so it is shared and has fair-use limits.

Is the chat unlimited?

There is no fixed message cap by default, but the server has limited power. When several people chat at once, replies queue, and each account has a per-minute limit to keep things fair. The administrator can also give an account extra free usage, or set a daily allowance, for a period of time.

Can Garo read or create images?

Yes, both. Read: attach a picture in a chat (or press + then Understand Image), type your question and press send. Garo looks at the picture and answers using Moondream 3.1. Create: press + then Generate Image (or pick Garo Image in the model list), describe the picture and send. Garo draws it with FLUX.1 Schnell, saves it to My files and shows a Download button. Garo only uses an AI model on a picture when you press send. Videos are not supported yet.

Which models does Garo use?

Five. Garo Chat, Garo Think and Garo Code run on GARO AI's private server for text. Garo Image (FLUX.1 Schnell) creates pictures and Garo Vision (Moondream 3.1) looks at pictures. Both picture models run on Cloudflare Workers AI, always through GARO AI's own server, so no access keys are ever in your browser.

Are there limits on pictures?

Yes. The two picture models share one small free daily allowance, and each account also has its own daily limit. When the shared allowance is nearly used up, picture features pause for everyone and the app shows when they return (they come back automatically at 00:00 UTC, which is 1 AM in Nigeria). Normal chat, groups, private messages and sharing pictures with friends are not affected.

Do pictures I share with friends go to the AI?

No. Pictures you send in groups, private chats or to the Administrator are only stored and delivered. They never reach FLUX or Moondream. Only the two buttons that say Generate Image and Understand Image, or sending a picture you attached in a chat, use those models.

Can I message a group member privately?

Yes. Tap a member's name or picture in a group (or in Group info) to see their profile card, then press Message. You can also long-press a group message and choose Reply privately. Only the two of you can read a private chat, not other members and not group admins. You can block anyone from their profile.

What is "View once"?

When you send a picture in a group, a private chat or to the Administrator you can switch on View once. The receiver taps to open it one time, and it is then locked and erased from our storage soon after. No website can stop a screenshot or a photo of the screen, so only share what you trust the receiver with.

Who can change the group picture?

Only group admins and the owner. The person who creates a group is its owner and is always an admin. Anyone can tap the group picture to view it.

How do I contact support?

Open your profile, then Help, then Contact support, to chat with the Administrator inside Garo. Replies appear in your Administrator chat. You can also email GARO AI at garoai.003@gmail.com.

What is Developers mode?

It is a shared group where several people build one project together. Anyone can ask Garo to build or change files, save the result to the project, preview it, and download it. Admins can add people, lock the group and manage roles.

How does Deep Research cite my documents?

You add the documents and, if you like, each one's author, year and title. Garo writes from the documents and the app adds the in-text citations and the reference list in the style you choose. Without documents, Garo does standard research and will not invent references.

Is Garo up to date?

Yes. Garo's built-in knowledge stops at a cutoff date, but it can check the web. Garo searches automatically for current things like news, prices and scores, and whenever you tell it an answer is wrong or outdated. You can also turn on Web search from the + menu, or press "Verify with the web" under an answer. Garo then reads current sources through a private search service and shows them as links so you can check them yourself.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, any time, from your profile. It erases your account, chats and group messages. Accounts that break the Terms of Use can be suspended or deleted by an administrator.

Who can see my chats?

Other users cannot. Chats are stored in your account with per-user access rules. Private messages between two people can be read only by those two. The administrator can technically reach stored data for security and support. See the Privacy Policy.
